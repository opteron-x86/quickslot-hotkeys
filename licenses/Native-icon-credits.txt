Quickslot — item artwork

All 65 populated item-atlas cells use artwork supplied by the Quickslot author:
64 supplied drawings and one family alias (gun uses crossbow).
Original PNGs are retained in the development assets/icons/source directory.
Packing normalizes white RGB, preserves aspect ratios and adds clear padding.
The supplied scroll has a baked checkerboard; its white silhouette is extracted
in the atlas copy. Source files remain unchanged. No public artwork license is
assigned by the packing process.

The former nine borrowed placeholder images have been replaced. The runtime
and source releases contain no item artwork from I4 Weapon Icons Overhaul,
THICC, SkyUI or Oathvein's inventory icon library.

Jost Regular and Medium: Owen Earl / the Jost Project Authors.
SIL Open Font License: Jost-OFL.txt.
https://github.com/indestructible-type/Jost

Keyboard/controller artwork is read from the user's active
Interface/ImGuiIcons/Icons files. No keycap artwork is included in the runtime
package. Loader tests use synthetic PNG fixtures.

Frame artwork and fonts are separate assets; see Theme-pack-credits.txt.
